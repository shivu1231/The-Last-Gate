# THE LAST GATE — teaser video brief

## Deliverable
Create a cinematic teaser from footage of the existing playable website. This is a Big Hero 6 fan experience told through Hiro's viewpoint. Use the actual game environments, models, props and interactions. Do not invent levels, dialogue, enemies, mechanics or story events. Target 75–90 seconds, 1920×1080, 16:9, 30 or 60 fps. Keep a clean version without subtitles and an accessible version with dialogue captions. Avoid browser chrome, developer panels, chapter labels and team credits in the cut.

## Core story
Hiro reaches home, realizes Baymax has not followed, and chooses to return through an unstable portal. A combat chip revives Baymax but lets a hostile program take over. After the fight and displacement, Hiro repairs him, finds a regulator that can restrict his output to 35%, and follows the missing healthcare chip's last signal. At the old portal, Hiro has to stop Baymax, release the reader's lock, and physically haul him home. The healthcare chip restores his gentler white state.

The emotional thread is simple: Hiro refuses to leave Baymax behind. Engineering and action are consequences of that choice, not separate stories.

## Visual continuity
- Normal Baymax is white. Seating the red combat chip changes him to red/purple combat armour. Restoring the green healthcare chip returns him to white.
- Preserve one canonical body, chest bay and scale. Include readable legs and feet in standing/flying shots.
- The regulator retrieved from Bay 6 is the one fitted to his chest and controlled later. Show its physical mounting and wire connection.
- The red combat chip removed from his chest is the one inserted into the portal reader. Do not imply that it is the missing healthcare chip.
- The relay capture points to where the healthcare chip went. Removing the combat chip releases the reader's lock; the recorded healthcare-chip transmission provides the destination.
- The damaged dimension has displaced architecture, cool haze and unstable light. Home is a grounded waterfront with railings, water, a bridge and warm dusk light.

## Full sequence and capture list

### Opening / portal return
Capture the unstable portal, the crossing toward San Fransokyo, Hiro turning back and seeing that Baymax has not followed. Hold briefly on the decision to return, then the dangerous crossing back. Use the opening-eye animation as a transition into the playable perspective. Following the eye opening, a brief translucent title treatment can retain the world behind THE LAST GATE and show the actual mouse/scroll controls.
Useful existing dialogue: “Baymax? …Baymax!” / “He’s not moving.” / “I’m not leaving you.”

### Phase 1 / combat activation and fight
Start with silent white Baymax. Show the red chip, the chest bay opening and the chip seating. Let the armour change and eyes wake register before cutting to danger. Select a close fly-by, charged ranged misses striking the environment and the heavy landing. Cut on acceleration, discharge and impact; retain enough wide footage to understand Baymax's direction and weight. Finish with the fracture pulling Hiro and Baymax away and one believable structural collision.
Useful existing dialogue: “The combat chip is still in you.” / “The combat chip is taking over.” / “That’s not him.”

### Phase 2 / fracture and trace, where available
The standalone legacy fracture/afterimage route shows the broken dimension and a scan of a still trace. It remains a development/legacy route, rather than a required extra chapter in the current normal playthrough. If this footage is included, treat it as a brief image of dimensional instability; do not claim the normal player must complete an additional level.
Existing dialogue: “He is mapping the dimension.”

### Recovery / engineering
Show the journey toward Baymax, then his supported semi-reclined pose with feet and head readable. Move into the actual chest/internal engineering view. Capture power, joint, thruster and sensor repairs as tactile close-ups. Keep this montage short but legible: damaged hardware, a deliberate repair, a response.
Existing dialogue: “Baymax… what happened to you?”

### Phase 4 / city and Bay 6
Capture the live cable leading through the damaged city, the approach to Bay 6, a short equipment inspection, restoration of the feed, the storage case opening and the regulator reveal. Preserve the current Bay 6 design. Contrast a quiet search with a small, promising mechanical discovery.
Useful existing dialogue: “I need something that slows him down.” / “That cable’s still live.” / “PR-6. Oh, you’re perfect.” / “It won’t fix him. But it’ll hold him.”

### Phase 5 / regulator fitting and telemetry
Show the regulator lifting, aligning, seating on Baymax and attaching its wire. No added Hiro model is needed in this shot. Capture the 35% output test while the combat program stays active. Then assemble a fast relay montage: power circuits, patch connections, interference filtering, receiver alignment and the saved transmission. The relay discovery drives the return to the old portal.
Use actual interface readouts such as 35% when visible. Do not turn interface copy into invented spoken dialogue.

### Phase 6 / pursuit and old portal
Open with Baymax entering at a readable distance. Show the remote, limited output, short thruster bursts and pursuit. Include an environmental miss and Hiro reaching the portal power rings. At the confrontation, show full output returning when the regulator is switched off, then the airborne cutoff when switched back on. Hold enough of the descent and road impact to convey weight. Follow with the chest bay opening, removal of the red combat chip, its insertion into the reader and the portal response.
Useful existing dialogue: “Baymax? Hey, buddy. It’s me.” / “Don’t look back. Don’t look back.” / “I’m not leaving you again.” / “Wait for it.” / “It’s reading his combat chip. That’s all it can hear.”

### Homecoming / restoration
Show Hiro dragging Baymax through the gate with visible contact and weight. Establish the waterfront beyond the portal. Include a glimpse of the healthcare chip near the bin, its retrieval and placement in Baymax's chest. End on the restored white Baymax and the existing gentle final character interaction. For a spoiler-light teaser, cut before the healthcare-chip placement and save the restoration for a separate full-story video.
Useful existing dialogue: “Come on. We’re going home.” / “Almost. Almost.” / “That green light… over by the bin?”
